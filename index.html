01 — About

An editor who cares about pacing, sound and the last frame.

I'm John Miles M. Gonzaga, a video editor working with companies and institutions since 2015. I cut short-form social content, documentaries and event films — taking a project from raw footage through assembly, pacing, titles, color and sound. My documentary work placed 1st at Division Level, and photojournalism 2nd.

Away from the timeline I draw in pencil. That habit is where the rest of it comes from: framing a shot, reading light, and knowing which frame to hold and which to cut.
