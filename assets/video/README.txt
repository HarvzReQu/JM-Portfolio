Put self-hosted video files here.

The gallery expects:
  showreel.mp4      — the tile labelled "Editing showreel" in index.html

Tips
  - MP4 (H.264 + AAC) plays everywhere. Keep it under ~20 MB for fast loading;
    1080p at a moderate bitrate is plenty for a portfolio.
  - The poster frame shown in the grid is assets/img/video-showreel.jpg — replace
    that with a still from the video.
  - For long videos, YouTube is lighter on your host: set data-type="youtube" and
    data-id="<video id>" on the tile instead, and no file is needed here.
